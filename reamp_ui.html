Battery Calculator
What is this for?

It estimates how long a battery would run the device you measured: battery life = battery capacity ÷ average current. Example: 55 mAh ÷ 0.00218 mA ≈ 25,200 hours ≈ 2 years 10 months.

  • Predict battery life of a design or a product, such as the watch on its two 395 cells.
  • Compare firmware versions, settings or operating modes by the battery life each would give.
  • Find what costs the most: put the markers around one part of the cycle and compare it with the whole period.
  • Size a battery: enter a current under User defined and try different capacities.

For a trustworthy average, measure long enough to include every repeating event, and average over whole cycles. The watch ticks every second and does something extra once a minute, so use whole minutes (By working cycle = 60, or markers exactly N minutes apart).

Real batteries usually last less than this estimate: the rated capacity is measured at a set load and cut-off voltage, it drops in the cold, self-discharge slowly drains a cell over a multi-year life, and the device may stop working before the battery is empty. Below 10 µA the REAMP is outside its rated range, so confirm such averages with a meter.

Voltage digitsClick a digit, then ↑ ↓ change it, ← → move, 0–9 type, Enter apply, Esc undo. The mouse wheel over a digit changes it.
ChartDrag to zoom into a span, mouse wheel to zoom, double-click to fit. Drag the T1/T2 flags to measure.
Y axesDrag up / down on an axis to zoom that trace in / out (mouse wheel too; Shift+drag moves it; double-click = automatic). Drag sideways, or click the arrow under it, to move it left or right.
